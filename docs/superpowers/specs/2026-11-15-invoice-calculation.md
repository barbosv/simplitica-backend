# Shared invoice calculation contract

Continue the fully authorized Workers/D1 integration without milestone approval pauses. This is the numeric contract for new connected drafts; records, tools and native synchronization follow continuously. Public access still requires the pending provider/device gates, and production deployment/migration/merge remains unauthorized.

## Chosen approach

Use bounded scaled BigInt arithmetic on the server and Foundation Decimal on iOS, with identical committed golden fixtures. A new decimal library is unnecessary for nonnegative fixed-scale USD arithmetic. Floating-point authoritative money and silently changing the historical native calculator are rejected. Existing InvoiceDraft uses Decimal but sums unrounded line extensions; leave historical documents and its existing calculation behavior unchanged until an explicit new-version integration.

Version `usd-line-cents-v1`, currency `USD`. Inputs contain 1–200 ordered lines of decimal-string quantity/unitPrice, decimal-string discountAmount and taxRatePercent. Exact object shapes; no authoritative caller totals. Accept ASCII digits with optional decimal fraction, normalize leading zeros/trailing fractional zeros; reject signs (including negative zero), whitespace, exponent notation, separators, non-string values and missing/extra fields. Each decimal input is at most 32 characters. Quantity is positive, at most 9 significant integer digits and 6 fractional digits; unitPrice is nonnegative with the same digit/scale bounds. Discount is nonnegative with at most 12 significant integer digits and 2 fractional digits. Tax rate is 0–100 inclusive, at most 4 fractional digits. Excess fractional digits are rejected even when zeros. All zeros normalize consistently.

Calculate each exact quantity×unitPrice and round half up to cents; sum those rounded line cents, cap requested discount to subtotal, subtract it, calculate tax on that taxable amount and round half up to cents, then add tax. Nonnegative authoritative line/subtotal/taxable/tax/total amounts must not exceed `999999999999.99`; overflow is a fixed error, never wrapping or rounding through binary floating point. Requested discount respects its input bound even when applied discount is smaller. Free lines and zero totals are valid draft arithmetic. This is owner-configured arithmetic, not a jurisdictional tax engine.

Return normalized quantity/tax-rate strings without unnecessary fractional zeros; unitPrice retains at least two fractional places and otherwise exact permitted precision. All money totals and discount strings have exactly two places. Output includes calculationVersion, currency, ordered normalized lines with lineTotal, subtotal, discountRequested, discountApplied, taxRatePercent, taxableAmount, taxAmount and total. No BigInt, Decimal, NaN or binary money value crosses JSON.

Required examples include 200×4 minus50 at6% => subtotal800.00, appliedDiscount50.00, taxable750.00, tax45.00,total795.00; three separately rounded0.335 lines =>subtotal1.02;0.5×0.01=>0.01;0.333333×0.015=>0.00. Cover rounding ties, normalization equivalence, zero tax/free lines, capped discount, limits and overflow in shared literal fixtures. Calculators are pure and cannot reserve numbers or persist drafts.

The native implementation is a reusable local Swift package with real executed tests, independent of the inherited main-app MLKit simulator blocker. Matching fixture SHA256 and output proves this package's parity, not main-app wiring. Later native integration must record the calculation version and preserve finalized historical amounts/customer snapshots rather than recomputing them.
